For founders and executives · iPhone

Your day, thought through before it starts.

Aforeday is a chief of staff for your working day. It reads the calendar and reminders already on your iPhone, tells you the few things that need you, and proposes what to do about them. Nothing happens until you approve it, and what it does can be undone.

Download on the App Store What Aforeday reads

Available worldwide on the App Store. A subscription is required; the price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you subscribe.

What it does

Three jobs a good chief of staff does, and nothing else.

  1. Your day, before it starts.

    Each morning Aforeday gives you a short list of what needs you today and what happens if you skip it. Not a summary of your day. Before each meeting it prepares a brief from your own calendar: who is in the room, when you last met them, and what you have outstanding. When nothing needs you, it says so and stays quiet.

  2. A calendar that pushes back.

    Aforeday tells the meetings you chose apart from the meetings booked onto you, and drafts the decline or the request to move it. You read the draft, and you send it.

  3. Propose. Approve. Act, with undo.

    Aforeday can work on what it shows you: it proposes an action, such as a reminder on your phone or a reply to review. Every proposal waits for you. If you choose “Act and tell me” for a kind of action, it tells you first and holds anything that leaves your phone for five minutes, with Undo in the notification. Turning that on needs Face ID or your passcode.

Your day, in one line.

The timeline puts what you have to do and where you need to be side by side, so the day reads in the order it will happen.

Ask what needs you.

The assistant answers from your own calendar and reminders, and its answers link back to the items they came from, so you can check them rather than trust them.

Quiet by default, and under your control.

Choose how much a notification reveals on your Lock Screen, and decide how Aforeday acts: ask you first for everything, which is the default, or act on reversible things like adding a reminder and tell you so you can undo it. Pause stops the agent reading, drafting and proposing at any time.

Your data

What Aforeday reads.

Aforeday works from what is already on your iPhone. It asks for calendar and reminder access, explains each request before iOS shows it, and you choose which calendars and lists count. There is no account to connect today: mail and chat accounts are not yet available.

Everything Aforeday can read, and what it keeps.

SourceWhat it is forWhat Aforeday keeps
Your iPhone’s calendars The timeline, the morning shortlist, meeting briefs, and telling meetings booked onto you from meetings you chose. For the calendars you include: each event’s title, start and end, time zone, whether it is all-day, and the display names of attendees. Notes, links and addresses stay on your phone.
Your iPhone’s reminders Showing open to-dos alongside your day, and adding one when you approve a proposal. For the lists you include: each reminder’s title and due date. Reminders that are open, or were completed in the last seven days.
What you tell it Answering your questions, and writing the drafts and notes you ask for. Your conversations with the assistant for 30 days, and drafts for 30 days or until you delete them.

What Aforeday never does. It never sends anything without your approval. It never asks for your location, contacts, photos or health data. Your voice is transcribed on your phone and the audio never leaves it; Face ID is checked by iOS and never leaves it either. It never uses your data for advertising, never sells it, and does not use it to train AI models. The details, including how long each kind of data is kept, are in the privacy policy.
