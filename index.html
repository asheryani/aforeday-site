Daily assistant · iPhone

The day, already in view.

Aforeday is a daily assistant for iPhone. It works from the calendar and reminders on your phone, keeps places in view, can speak a reply, and prepares drafts for you to approve.

What it does

A few jobs, done quietly.

You talk. Aforeday keeps the day in view and waits when a decision is yours.

01

A reply, spoken.

Aforeday can speak a reply. The reply text is turned into audio in Frankfurt, and we do not keep that audio. The live Talk session is not on.

02

Calendar and reminders.

Both come from your iPhone through Apple EventKit, from the calendars and lists you allow.

03

Places, when they matter.

Location is used while you are using the app. Coordinates stay on the iPhone. A leave-by time can be suggested without sending your position.

04

On the Lock Screen.

Live Activities can show a meeting that is about to start, or that it is time to leave. A focus session stays on the iPhone.

05

Drafts, for your approval.

Aforeday prepares a draft. The phone does not send it: it can open the Gmail app, Mail, or Messages, or copy the text. Mail and chat accounts are not connected.

06

A focus session.

Starting a focus session keeps that record on the iPhone, with a local notification when it ends. Evening close-out and Share capture are not on.

In the app

Morning, a reply, a draft, a place.

Privacy

In Frankfurt, and on the phone.

The backend and the AI run on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, eu-central-1. Coordinates stay on the iPhone.

European Union

Text uses Amazon Nova 2 Lite, model amazon.nova-2-lite-v1:0, through the EU inference profile eu.amazon.nova-2-lite-v1:0, called from Frankfurt (eu-central-1). A spoken reply’s text is sent to Amazon Polly in Frankfurt. We do not keep that audio.

Your voice

Talk mode is not on, so a live voice session is not sent to Stockholm. You sign in with Apple or with Google. Google is asked only for openid, email, and profile.

Your places

Location is When In Use only. Coordinates do not leave the iPhone. The privacy policy lists what is still open.

Pricing

Pro Monthly. Pro Annual.

There is no free trial. Apple bills the subscription. We never see your card. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you subscribe.

Aforeday Pro

Two billing periods. The same subscription.

  • Pro Monthly or Pro Annual. No free trial.
  • Renews until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the period ends, in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • You keep access until the period you paid for ends. Refunds are requested from Apple.
  • Restore Purchases brings a subscription you already have to another iPhone.
