Talk through the day ahead.

Aforeday is a voice-first daily assistant. You talk to it. Text is there when you want it. It works from the calendar and reminders on your iPhone, notices places, and prepares drafts for you to approve.

What it does

A few jobs, done quietly.

You talk. Aforeday keeps the day in view and waits when a decision is yours.

01

You talk. Text can wait.

Tap to talk. Typing is optional. The recording and the speech recognition stay on your iPhone, and the audio is not uploaded.

02

Calendar and reminders.

Both come from your iPhone through Apple EventKit, from the calendars and lists you allow.

03

Places, when they matter.

Location-aware reminders and suggestions: a nudge when you arrive or leave, and when it is time to go.

04

On the Lock Screen.

Dynamic Island and Live Activities keep something time-sensitive in view, such as when to leave or a focus session that is running.

05

Drafts, for your approval.

Aforeday prepares a draft and stops there. You approve it. The screen says nothing is sent until you do.

06

Focus, then the evening.

A focus session for a block of time, and an evening close-out when the day is done. The share extension lets another app hand something to Aforeday.

In the app

Morning, a reply, a draft, a place.

Privacy

In Frankfurt, and on the phone.

The backend and the AI run on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, eu-central-1. The microphone recording does not.

European Union

Servers and AI run in Frankfurt. AI uses Amazon Bedrock. A spoken reply’s text is sent to Amazon Polly there, to be turned into audio. Neither Polly nor we store that audio. If Polly fails, the iPhone speaks with its own voice.

Your voice

Speech recognition stays on the iPhone. The audio itself is not uploaded. You sign in with Apple or with Google. Google is asked only for openid, email, and profile.

Your places

Location is used for place-based reminders and suggestions. The privacy policy says what we could confirm, and what still needs a check against the app.

Pricing

Pro Monthly. Pro Annual.

There is no free trial. Apple bills the subscription. We never see your card. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you subscribe.

Aforeday Pro

Two billing periods. The same subscription.

  • Pro Monthly or Pro Annual. No free trial.
  • Renews until you cancel, at least 24 hours before the period ends, in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • You keep access until the period you paid for ends. Refunds are requested from Apple.
  • Restore Purchases brings a subscription you already have to another iPhone.
