Privacy policy

For the Aforeday iPhone app and this website. Last updated 12 November 2026, version 3.2. It replaces version 3.1 of 3 November 2026. It describes the app as it works on that date. A passage marked “TODO for review” is not yet confirmed against the current source and should be settled before this version is published.

The short version

  • Aforeday is a voice-first assistant. The microphone recording and the speech recognition stay on your iPhone. The audio is not uploaded.
  • To speak a reply, the reply text is sent to Amazon Polly in Frankfurt. Neither Polly nor we store that audio. If that fails, the iPhone speaks with its own voice.
  • You sign in with Apple or Google. Google is asked only for openid, email, and profile.
  • Calendar and reminders are read through Apple EventKit, from the calendars and lists you allow.
  • Location is used for place-based reminders and suggestions. Section 2 marks what we could not re-check in the code.
  • Aforeday prepares drafts for your approval.
  • The backend and the AI run on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt (eu-central-1). AI uses Amazon Bedrock.
  • Aforeday Pro is Pro Monthly or Pro Annual, billed by Apple. There is no free trial. We never see your card.
  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from anyone else.

2. What Aforeday processes

Your account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or with Google. With Apple, we receive the account identifier Apple issues for Aforeday, the email address Apple gives us (this may be a private relay address if you hide yours), and your name if Apple provides it.

With Google, the app asks only for openid, email, and profile. Your phone completes the sign-in with Google and sends us Google’s signed confirmation of who you are. From that confirmation we store your Google account identifier, your email address, and your name. Those three permissions do not include Gmail, Google Calendar, or any other Google product. If Google confirms an address that matches an Aforeday account you already have, the Google sign-in is added to that account.

Aforeday is voice-first. Text is optional. The microphone recording and the speech recognition stay on your iPhone. The audio itself is not uploaded. The words speech recognition produces are sent to our servers in Frankfurt so Aforeday can answer and prepare a draft. You can also type. What you type is sent in the same way.

With your permission, the app reads the calendars and reminder lists you choose through Apple EventKit. You can change that choice in the app or in your iPhone’s Settings.

4. AI and spoken replies

The assistant, including drafts and the help it prepares for your day, uses Amazon Bedrock. Requests are made from our servers in Frankfurt (eu-central-1).

When Aforeday speaks a reply, the reply text is sent to Amazon Polly, using generative voices, in Frankfurt, to be turned into audio. Neither Amazon Polly nor we store that audio. If Polly cannot produce it, the iPhone speaks the reply with its own on-device voice, and the reply text is not sent to Polly for that attempt.

We do not use what is sent to the model to train any model of our own, and no part of our code trains or fine-tunes a model.

6. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app — the processing in sections 2 to 4. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Other people in your data — names of people on the calendars and reminders you allow, and names in drafts you ask for. We process them only so Aforeday can show your day and prepare a draft for you. Legal basis: the legitimate interest, yours and ours, in providing the service you asked for, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To keep the service secure and working — logs, abuse prevention, and request limits. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning service, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To prove we honoured your choices — records of consent and of deletion, where we keep them. Legal basis: our legal obligation to demonstrate compliance, Article 6(1)(c) with Article 5(2) GDPR.
  • To sell and service subscriptions — the subscription records Apple’s notifications allow us to keep. Legal basis: performance of our contract, and our legal obligations in accounting.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Suggestions and drafts can be wrong. They do not decide anything for you.

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple, push notifications, Live Activities, and the App Store when you subscribe. On the iPhone: speech recognition, the on-device voice used when Polly fails, and the Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode check when the app asks iOS to confirm it is you. iOS performs that check. We receive only whether it succeeded.
  • Google, for sign-in — if you sign in with Google, your phone talks to Google, and Google confirms to us who you are. Google keeps its own record of that sign-in under its own privacy policy. The permissions are only those in section 2.

8. Where it is processed

The app’s servers and its AI run on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1). That is where Aforeday stores what it keeps, and where Amazon Bedrock is called from. Spoken-reply text is sent to Amazon Polly in the same region. The resulting audio is not stored by Polly or by us.

Apple’s push, sign-in, Live Activities, and App Store services are operated by Apple under its own terms and may involve processing outside the EU. Google sign-in is operated by Google under its own terms and may involve processing outside the EU. Where that happens, the transfer takes place on the transfer mechanisms in that provider’s terms, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Cancel that in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Aforeday from your Google Account’s list of third-party connections.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Article 15 GDPR), to correct it (16), to have it erased (17), to restrict its processing (18), to receive it in a portable form (20), and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests (21). Export and deletion in the app are how you use access, portability, and erasure directly. For anything else, write to contact@aforeday.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. Ours is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.

13. Children

Aforeday is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 to use it, and we do not knowingly process the data of anyone younger.

14. This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party fonts or scripts, and stores nothing about you. Fonts are the Apple system font or Inter files served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address and the request in its logs in order to deliver the pages, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

15. Changes

If Aforeday starts collecting something new, or uses what it has for a new purpose, we update this policy first and change the date at the top. Where the app shows a notice of a new version, it records that you have seen it.
