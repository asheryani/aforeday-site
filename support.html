Getting started

What do I need?
An iPhone. You install Aforeday from the App Store and subscribe with your Apple ID. In the app, sign in with Apple or with Google, then allow the calendars and reminders you want. Google sign-in asks only for openid, email, and profile. It does not connect Gmail or Google Calendar. You can talk, or you can type.
Why does it ask for calendar and reminders?
Aforeday reads them through Apple EventKit, from the calendars and lists you allow. You can change that in the app or in your iPhone’s Settings. The privacy policy says what is still to be confirmed about which fields leave the phone.
Does it use my location?
Yes, while you are using the app, for place-based reminders and for when to leave. It asks for When In Use, not Always. Coordinates stay on the iPhone. What we receive is a leave-by time, not a position. The privacy policy describes the rest.
What about focus, the evening, and Share?
A focus session is kept on the iPhone. Evening close-out and Share capture are switched off. Live Activities can show a meeting that is starting or that it is time to leave.

Voice

Where does my voice go?
A spoken reply’s text is sent to Amazon Polly in Frankfurt. We do not keep that audio. Talk mode, the live voice session, is switched off, so it is not sent to Stockholm. The privacy policy says what is still open about ordinary dictation.
A draft appeared that I did not want to send.
Aforeday prepares drafts for you to approve. Nothing is sent until you approve it. If something still looks wrong, write to us with the time and what was on the screen.

Subscription

What are the plans?
Aforeday Pro Monthly and Aforeday Pro Annual. There is no free trial. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you subscribe. Apple charges your Apple ID. We never see your card.
How do I cancel?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Aforeday. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep access until the period you paid for is over.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I already subscribed on another iPhone.
Sign in on this iPhone with the same Apple ID and use Restore Purchases.

Your data

How do I export or delete my data?
Open Settings › Privacy › Privacy & data. “Export my data” saves a JSON file on the iPhone. “Delete account” asks you to type DELETE, then “Delete everything”. “Keep my account” cancels. You need to have signed in recently. Deleting the account does not cancel the App Store subscription. The privacy policy describes this, including what is still open about the file’s contents.

Security

If you believe you have found a security problem in Aforeday, write to contact@aforeday.com with “Security report” in the subject, and do not share it publicly until we have had a chance to fix it.
