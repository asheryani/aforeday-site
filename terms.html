3. What Aforeday does, and what it does not

Aforeday reads the calendars and reminder lists you allow through Apple EventKit, uses location while you use the app for place-based reminders and for when to leave, and can show a meeting or a leave-by on a Live Activity. A focus session stays on the iPhone. Some answers and drafts are written with Amazon Nova 2 Lite on Amazon Bedrock, model amazon.nova-2-lite-v1:0, through the EU inference profile eu.amazon.nova-2-lite-v1:0, from Frankfurt (eu-central-1). A spoken reply’s text may be sent to Amazon Polly in Frankfurt. Talk mode, evening close-out, and Share capture are switched off. The privacy policy describes what stays on the iPhone and what is sent.

Aforeday is an aid to your own judgement. Its suggestions, drafts, and spoken replies can be incomplete or wrong. You remain responsible for your decisions and for anything you send. The phone does not send a draft as mail. It can open the Gmail app, a mail link, or a message link, or copy the text. Under “What Aforeday may do on its own” you can choose “Ask me first” or “Act and tell me”. Choosing “Act and tell me” asks for Face ID or the passcode. Mail and Slack connectors are switched off, so the app does not deliver mail or Slack messages.
